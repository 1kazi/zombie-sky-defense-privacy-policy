01 / The short version

Overview

Zombie Sky Defense is an offline mobile arcade game in which a pilot defends against descending waves of zombies. The game currently has no player accounts and does not send gameplay information to a server.

There are currently no ads, analytics tools, or in-app purchases integrated into the game. We do not sell or share gameplay data because the game does not collect it through online services.

◉ No gameplay connection

Gameplay does not make requests to our servers.

▰ Progress stays on device

Saved game progress is kept locally on your device.

02 / Information

Information we collect

The game does not ask you to create an account or provide personal information to play. It does not currently transmit gameplay data, device identifiers, or other game usage information to us.

Your device platform or app store may process information under its own terms when you download or update the game. That activity is controlled by the platform provider, not by the game’s offline gameplay, and is not covered by this policy.

03 / Saved locally

Progress and local storage

Zombie Sky Defense uses AsyncStorage on your device to save game progress, including your highest level, high score, total kills, and stars. This information is stored locally for the game to remember your progress between sessions. It is not sent to a game server.

Removing the game or clearing its app data may erase locally saved progress. Device backup, migration, or operating-system behavior may also affect local app data; we do not control those platform features.

04 / Connections

Network use and third parties

As currently built, gameplay does not make server requests. The game does not currently include advertising, analytics, or in-app purchase integrations. We therefore do not use those services to collect information through the game at this time.

Game audio is bundled with the app and played locally on your device; audio is not streamed from a service.

If these features or other network-connected services are added later, this policy will need to be updated before those features are introduced, including clear information about any data they handle.

05 / Younger players

Children’s privacy

The game does not require an account or ask players to submit personal information, and it does not currently transmit gameplay information to us. Because we do not operate an online data-collection service in the game, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay.

If you have a concern about information associated with the game, please contact us using the address below.

06 / Safeguards

Security and retention

Saved progress remains in the app’s local storage on your device. We do not receive or retain that progress on a game server. The security and backup of information stored on your device also depend on your device, operating system, and settings.

You can remove local game data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it, subject to your device platform’s behavior.

07 / Your control

Your choices

You can play without creating an account. You can clear locally stored progress through your device’s app settings or by uninstalling the game. Doing so may permanently remove your high score, level, kills, and stars.

08 / Keeping this current

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if the game changes or if the way it handles information changes. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version. In particular, this policy must be updated before ads or analytics are added, and before any new feature begins collecting or transmitting information.
